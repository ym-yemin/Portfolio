Renewable energy and energy efficiency are central to developing sustainable energy systems. As more wind and solar generation enters electricity networks, however, balancing supply and demand becomes increasingly complex.

Digitalization provides one way to coordinate a growing number of distributed energy resources.

## What is a virtual power plant?

A Virtual Power Plant, or VPP, combines multiple distributed resources and coordinates them so that they can operate as a single aggregated system.

These resources may include:

- solar and wind generation,
- battery storage,
- electric vehicles,
- dispatchable generators, and
- flexible electricity demand.

## Digital coordination

Smart meters, communications infrastructure and software enable a VPP operator to receive information from distributed assets and respond to changing electricity-system conditions.

Weather forecasts, power output, market prices and electricity demand can be combined to determine when resources should generate, store or consume electricity.

## Unlocking flexibility

The major value of a VPP is flexibility. Thousands of small resources that would individually have little influence on a wholesale electricity system can become significant when coordinated.

Aggregation can help integrate renewable generation, support grid stability and provide additional opportunities for distributed resources to participate in electricity markets.

## Connection to sustainable development

Virtual power plants can contribute to wider sustainable development objectives by enabling more renewable energy, supporting innovation and infrastructure development and reducing emissions.

Their successful deployment will require appropriate regulation, secure communication systems, viable electricity markets, better storage technologies and meaningful community participation.
