01 / About

Engineering the energy transition.

My work sits at the intersection of offshore wind, power systems and energy policy, with a focus on the practical challenges of the energy transition.

I work in offshore wind and renewable energy, with a particular interest in how technology, power systems and policy come together to shape the energy transition.

Currently, I am an Offshore Wind Energy Technical Analyst at SSE Pacifico K.K. My academic background includes graduate study at Kyoto University and the University of Malaya, where I developed a broader interest in renewable-energy systems and sustainable development.

Beyond my professional work, I write about offshore wind, electricity markets, grid integration, energy policy and the wider challenges of building reliable, affordable and low-carbon energy systems. This site is a place to share those ideas, explore questions I find important, and document what I continue to learn along the way.
